How It Began

From a Single Unit to a Packaging Powerhouse

The existence of Maqsood Faisal Printing Press is merely based on the struggle to commensurate towards the Nation building. What started 30 years back from a single printing unit in Offset Printing is now an irrefutable packaging complex.

1989 • The Vision

Late Chairman Haji Maqsood Ahmed stepped ahead to establish a Printing Press & its Conversion into Packaging material by using indigenous resources in a judicious way.

Growth • Expansion

Coping with the technology-based invasion, mass production is carried out, encompassing Metallized, Rotogravure, Offset, Labelling, Tube making, Pouch making, and laminates.

Today

Now an SMC-Pvt. Ltd. — a family-oriented entity led by CEO Mr. Mahmood Ahmed. MFP is recognized by diversified clienteles and is a member of the Lahore Chamber of Commerce & Industry.
