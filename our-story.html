How It Began

From a Single Unit to a Packaging Powerhouse

Since 1990, we've grown from a single offset printing unit to a six-division packaging powerhouse — trusted by Pakistan's leading brands.

1990 • Foundation

Late Chairman Haji Maqsood Ahmed launched our first offset printing unit on 18-Km Ferozepur Road, Lahore.

2000s • Growth

Expanded into Labels, Flexible Packaging, Tissue Conversion, Plastic Packaging & Laminated Tubes.

Today

Now SMC-Pvt. Ltd. — ISO 9001:2015 & FSSC 22000 certified, serving Food, Pharma, FMCG, and more.
