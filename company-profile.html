Who We Are

Maqsood Faisal Printing Press

Maqsood Faisal Printing Press (SMC-Pvt.) Ltd. was established in 1990 by our late Chairman, Haji Maqsood Ahmed, with a single offset printing unit and a vision to revolutionize Pakistan's printing and packaging industry. What began as a modest venture has grown into one of the country's most trusted names in premium packaging solutions.

Over three decades, we have continuously expanded our capabilities across Offset Printing, Roll-to-Roll Labels, Flexible Packaging, Tissue Conversion, Plastic Packaging, and Laminated Tubes. Our state-of-the-art facility at 18-Km Ferozepur Road, Lahore, serves a diverse clientele of leading national and international brands.

We are certified for ISO 9001:2015 and FSSC 22000, reflecting our unwavering commitment to quality management and food safety. Our philosophy — "Emerging Quality through Design" — drives every project we undertake, from concept to delivery.

Today, we are expanding into Blown Film Extrusion and a Tissue Paper Convertor at Allama Iqbal Industrial Estate (SEZ of CPEC), reinforcing our position as a future-ready packaging partner for industries including Food, Pharmaceuticals, Personal & Home Care, Agriculture, and Construction.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

What drives us

1. To be a leader in the printing and packaging markets we serve by providing premium quality products and superior service to our customers while learning from their feedback to set even higher standards.

2. To continuously enhance our technological competence — from offset printing to flexible packaging — to deliver innovative solutions that meet evolving customer needs.

3. To attract and retain outstanding people by fostering a culture of openness, innovation, and individual growth, rewarding initiative and performance.

4. To combine our people, technology, management systems, and market opportunities to achieve profitable growth while providing fair returns to our stakeholders.

5. To set the highest standards of corporate ethics and environmental responsibility in serving our society and community.

Our Vision

Where we aim to be

1. To position Maqsood Faisal Printing Press as a regional leader in quality packaging and consumer products across South Asia.

2. To improve on contemporary measures including cost, quality, service, speed of delivery, and mobilization in every project we deliver.

3. To keep investing in technology, systems, and human resource to effectively meet the challenges of a rapidly evolving packaging industry.

4. To develop relationships with all our stakeholders based on sustainable cooperation and ethical values — upheld by our shareholders, management, and employees alike.

Principle Business

Business & Other Activities

Maqsood Faisal Printing Press (SMC-Pvt.) Ltd. is a leading Pakistani printing and packaging company offering end-to-end solutions across six core divisions: Offset Printing (folding cartons, commercial printing), Roll-to-Roll Labels (self-adhesive labels, shrink sleeves), Flexible Packaging (pouches, roll films), Tissue Conversion (napkins, facial tissues, paper towels), Plastic Packaging (blown film, extrusion), and Laminated Tubes (cosmetics, pharma, food). Our client base includes some of the world's best-known FMCG, pharmaceutical, and personal care brands. We are also expanding into Blown Film Extrusion and Tissue Paper Conversion at Allama Iqbal Industrial Estate — SEZ of CPEC — to meet growing demand and strengthen our position as a future-ready packaging partner.
